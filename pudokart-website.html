Everything between your supplier and your customer

Use one service or the whole chain. You keep selling; we handle the floor space and the parcels.

Warehousing and storage

Secure space for your goods, so you don’t need to rent, staff or manage a warehouse of your own.

Inventory management

We receive, count and shelve your stock, and keep records that match what is physically on the shelf.

Pick, pack and dispatch

Orders are picked from your stock, packed and handed over for delivery on the schedule you agree with us.

Pickup and drop-off

We collect goods from your supplier or location and deliver to your customers or partners.

Returns handling

Returned parcels come back to us, are checked, and go back into stock or to you, as you decide.

How working with us starts

  1. Tell us what you ship

    Share your products, volumes and where your orders go.

  2. Send stock in

    Goods arrive at our Delhi warehouse or we collect them.

  3. We run the floor

    Storage, picking, packing and dispatch are handled by our team.

  4. Adjust as you grow

    Add products, space or services when your business needs them.
